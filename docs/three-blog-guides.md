## Prompt set

Shared prompt: Use case: illustration-story. Create one polished landscape 1536x1024 editorial blog cover for a Belgian graphic-design studio. Warm ivory/beige lightly textured paper background, dark chocolate brown bold rounded outlines, muted teal and apricot orange flat fills, warm restrained retro illustration with generous space. In the top third set the exact Dutch heading in very large bold dark-brown sans-serif, two lines. In the bottom two thirds place the subject below. Keep all objects comfortably inside the frame. Clean simple graphic composition, flat illustration, no photorealism, no real client brands, no watermark, no other words or letters. Cohesive companion to warm beige graphic-design educational covers with a bold heading and outlined stationery objects.
